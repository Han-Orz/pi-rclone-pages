AgentDrive

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

About this tool

AgentDrive is a personal-use rclone setup operated by the account owner on a private server. The owner’s AI agent, Pi, can use it to carry out file operations in the owner’s Google Drive when the owner gives an instruction. It is not offered as a public service.

Google Drive data and its use

Depending on the requested operation, the tool accesses Drive files and related information such as file names, identifiers, and other file metadata. It uses that data to read, upload, download, or manage files in response to the owner’s instructions.

The tool does not use Google Drive data for purposes unrelated to those requested file operations.

Storage and sharing

OAuth configuration and tokens used by rclone are stored on the owner’s private server. File data may be read or written in Google Drive as part of an operation. Files downloaded, created, or changed are handled according to the requested operation and the services involved.

When the owner asks Pi to analyze, summarize, transform, or discuss a file, relevant file content or metadata may be sent to the AI model service Pi uses. Information may also be sent through or appear in the messaging platform used to communicate with Pi. Those services handle data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention and control

Drive files remain subject to the owner’s Google Drive account and any file operations the owner requests. Copies or conversation content handled by a model service or messaging platform are subject to that service’s settings and retention practices. This tool does not define or control those providers’ retention periods.

The owner can stop using the tool and revoke its Google account access through the Google Account’s third-party access settings.

Contact

This is a personal-use tool with no public support address. Privacy questions can be directed to the owner through the private channel used to interact with Pi.
